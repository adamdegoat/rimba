Two men. One cat. No way out.
Rimba is a never-ending story — two Singaporeans and a cat stranded deep in the Malaysian jungle after their 4x4 breaks down, with no script, no crew, and no rescue. The story unfolds five times a day. The characters change, harden, and surprise you as time goes on — no one controls them. Each night the jungle takes its turn — a storm, an injury, a discovery — that can shift the whole storyline. This entire world runs at zero cost.
